THE MASK & THE MIRROR

A Map of the Unconditioned Mind

Most tests measure the version of you that was built to survive inside a machine. They ask how you work, how you produce, and how you perform for a world that taught you to separate your mind from the Earth, your community, and your own voice.

This is not a test. It is a diagnostic of the soul.

We are going to ask you to make choices—not based on who you were taught to be, but on how your spirit reacts when stripped of the noise, the hurry, and the hypnosis of the modern grid.

By the end, this game will separate:

  • The Essence: Who you were before the world told you who to be
  • The Armor: The strategies you built to navigate the colonial apparatus
  • The Synthesis: Where your true power is currently leaking—and how to reclaim it
